Welcome to our locality

A land of nature,
people & stories.

Kovilmala, located in the Idukki district of Kerala, is known for its association with the Mannan community and its distinctive cultural heritage.

Surrounded by the landscapes of the Western Ghats, the locality offers a glimpse into the region's natural environment and community life.

This is a place where traditions, local identity, and everyday life come together to shape a unique story.

Our past

A history rooted
in tradition.

Kovilmala is closely associated with the Mannan community, an indigenous community of Kerala with its own cultural practices, traditions, and history.

The community is known for its traditional leadership system and its historical connection to the region. These traditions form an important part of Kovilmala's cultural identity.

The detailed history of the locality can be explored through oral histories, community accounts, and documented historical sources.
